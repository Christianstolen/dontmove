DON'T MOVE

How still can you hold it?

Hold your iPhone as still as you can. The round starts by itself, counts down, and measures how far the phone drifts. Your Drift Score is that drift in degrees. Lower is better.

  • Classic, Endurance and Sudden Death for your own records.
  • Daily: the same challenge for everyone, one official attempt a day.
  • Poses: harder ways to hold it, each with its own record.
  • Pass & Play: 2 to 8 players on one phone.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
