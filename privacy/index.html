Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Don't Move is made by Stølen Development AS, Norway, which is responsible for this policy. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: nothing leaves your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Don't Move has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Motion data. During a round the app reads your iPhone's motion sensors to calculate your Drift Score. This happens on the device, in real time. Raw sensor readings are not stored.
  • Your results. Scores, grades, the mode you played, the date and the player names you type in Pass & Play are saved on your iPhone so you can see your history and bests.
  • Your settings. Sound, haptics and display choices are saved on your iPhone.

Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include this app data, according to your device settings. Apple handles those backups.

Sharing

The app shares something only when you tap Share. It then hands an image and a short text to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete All Results to remove your history. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app holds no personal data about you on our side, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete. For email you have sent us, you can ask for access or deletion at any time. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date.
