Roadmap

What we are working on, without dates. Plans change; nothing here is a promise. Ideas and requests are welcome at main@stolendevelopment.no.

Now

  • Launch on the App Store for iPhone.
  • Challenge links: share a score as a link or QR code, and your friend plays against it.
  • How you held it: a breakdown of every round, with a replay against your best and a tip for the next try.

Next

  • Widgets for the Daily, your streak and your best.
  • More poses, starting with ones you can do sitting down.
  • More languages.

Later

  • Play together on several phones at once.
  • Game Center leaderboards, once we are sure scores are fair across iPhone models.

Never

  • Selling your data.
  • An account just to play.
