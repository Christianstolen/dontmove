Status

Known problems and their workarounds. Don't Move has no servers, so there is nothing that can be down: if the app opens, it works. This page is about the app itself.

Known issues

  • None reported. If something looks wrong, email main@stolendevelopment.no with your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version from Settings.

Good to know

  • A round was cancelled. Leaving the app, a call, or the phone dropping or moving too much before the start cancels the round on purpose, so a score is always a fair one.
  • My score is different on another iPhone. Sensors differ a little between models. Compare scores on the same phone.
  • The Daily says Used today. A Daily counts as soon as measuring starts, even if the round was interrupted. A new one comes at midnight.
